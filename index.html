About us

We are a publisher and seller of board games. The company has been operating since 2026 and provides distribution services for board games in Armenia. At the moment, we have one office in Yerevan, where we have a printing station next to the store. At the moment, our young publishing house is looking for game designers who could offer original ideas for our own real games, for more information, see the publishing services section. We also have an assortment of games of different genres, which can be found in the our products section. Our store provides delivery within Armenia.
